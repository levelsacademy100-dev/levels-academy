How to Choose the Best Home Tutor or Online Tutor in Pakistan

Choosing a tutor is an important decision for any family. A good home tutor builds understanding and confidence, while a poor match wastes time and money. After helping students since 2015, here is what we have learnt about finding the right teacher.

Home Tutor or Online Tutor: Which Is Better?

A home tutor in Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Sahiwal, Pakpattan or Arifwala is best for young children and students who need a teacher physically beside them. An online tutor is ideal when you cannot find a specialist nearby, for example an A Level Physics teacher in a small town. Online classes also save travel time and are very safe for girls.

Male or Female Tutor?

Both can be excellent. What matters is subject knowledge, teaching style and the comfort of the student. Many families choose a female home tutor for daughters and for primary classes, and a male tutor for senior science classes. At Levels Academy you can simply state your preference.

Match the Tutor to the Syllabus

A Matric teacher and a Cambridge teacher are not the same. For O Level, A Level and IGCSE, ask for a tutor who regularly teaches past papers and knows the mark scheme. For FSc and MDCAT, choose someone who covers both board concepts and MCQ practice. For private admission students, a tutor who understands the board pattern helps most.

Private Admission: Regular and Supply Exams

Private candidates can appear in Matric and FSc through BISE boards and Federal Board, either as regular students or in the supply (supplementary) exam to clear failed subjects. Cambridge students can register privately for O Level, A Level and IGCSE. Always check dates and fee with your board, and start preparation early. We guide you with the process and provide tutors for each subject.

Questions to Ask Before You Start

Ask about the tutor's qualification and experience, the teaching plan, the timing, how progress will be reported, and the monthly fee. A good tutor welcomes these questions. Start with a trial class and continue only when you and the student are happy.
