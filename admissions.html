Admission FAQs

Can I send private admission for any class and any subject?

Yes. Private students can contact Levels Academy for admission guidance in Matric (Class 9 and 10), Inter (Class 11 and 12: FSc, FA, ICS, ICom), O Level, A Level and IGCSE, in any subject, subject to the rules of the board.

Do you help with supply (supplementary) exam admission?

Yes. If you have failed or have compartment subjects, contact us and we will guide you about the supplementary exam admission process and help you submit your admission.

Which boards do you work with?

We guide private candidates of BISE boards, Federal Board (FBISE) and Cambridge O Level, A Level and IGCSE. If your board is different, message us and we will tell you if we can help.

No. Admission dates, fees and eligibility are decided by each board and can change. Contact us for the latest information before you apply.
