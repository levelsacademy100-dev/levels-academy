Exam Structure & Timetable

Complete exam schedules for all boards — Cambridge, Federal, and Punjab Boards (2026)

🎓

Cambridge (O/A Levels, IGCSE)

May/June 2026 Series: Exams held 23 April - 9 June 2026. Results announced 11 August 2026.

October/November 2026 Series: Exams held late September - early November 2026. Results announced January 2027.

Registration Deadlines: May/June entries close 21 February 2026. Oct/Nov normal entries close 6 August 2026; late entries 3 September 2026.

📚

Federal Board (FBISE)

Matric (SSC) Annual: Exams start 31 March 2026. Practicals from 7 May 2026.

Inter (HSSC) Annual: Exams start 14 May 2026.

Matric Supplementary: Admission forms (single fee) 29 July - 17 August 2026. Exams from September 2026.

📖

Punjab Boards (Sahiwal, etc.)

Matric Annual 2026: Exams start 27 March 2026. Results 6 August 2026.

Inter Annual 2026: Exams start 20 May 2026. Results 23 September 2026.

Matric Supplementary 2026: Exams start 6 October 2026. Results 8 December 2026.

Inter Supplementary 2026: Exams start 3 November 2026. Results 12 January 2027.

📋

Registration Deadlines

Cambridge: Contact us for current registration windows.

Federal Board: Inter admission forms (single fee) 07 Jan - 03 Feb 2026. Matric supplementary (single fee) 29 July - 17 August 2026.

Punjab Boards: Matric supplementary (single fee) 07 - 19 August 2026. Inter supplementary (single fee) 24 Sept - 6 Oct 2026.
