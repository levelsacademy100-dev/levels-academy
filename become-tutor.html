Tutor Registration FAQs

Who can apply as a tutor at Levels Academy?

Graduates, university students in their later years, and experienced teachers who are strong in at least one subject can apply. Both male and female tutors are welcome.

Is there a fee to register?

No. Please do not pay anyone for registration. If a family and tutor agree to start classes, the monthly fee is discussed clearly before the first lesson.

Can I teach online from home?

Yes. Choose online, home or both when you apply. Online tutors need a stable internet connection and a quiet place to teach on Zoom or Google Meet.

Do you need a demo video?

A short intro video is optional but helps. It lets us see how clearly you explain a topic and speeds up approval.

How soon will I hear back?

We try to contact shortlisted applicants on WhatsApp within a few days. After a short call and document check, your profile goes into our matching list.

Looking for a tutor instead?

Visit our Find a Tutor page, or see the cities we serve.
